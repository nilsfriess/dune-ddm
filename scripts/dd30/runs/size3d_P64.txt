# Cost against subdomain size, 3D: 64 ranks, the channels problem (contrast 1e6, velocity 1, ex6.ini geometry; the
# discrete channel width varies a little with the mesh) on cells^3 x 4^3 elements, so 12^3 .. 24^3 elements per
# subdomain at a fixed overlap of 4 layers. Threshold 0.5 for all coarse spaces, shift -1e-9 for the variants with
# the projection outside of the shifted solve. RANKS_PER_NODE=8: the saddle point method needs ~20 GB per rank at 24^3.
# Within a size the saddle point method runs last. Earlier ladder (size_3d.txt, checkerboard, nev 40, shift -1e-3):
# the ring's eigenproblems were 12/27/33% cheaper than inner_product subdomain at 16^3/20^3/24^3, and one rank of the
# saddle point method needed 483 Lanczos operator applications (instead of ~40) at 20^3
