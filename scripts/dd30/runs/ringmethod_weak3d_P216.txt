# The MS-GFEM-ring method of arXiv:2507.12226 (methods/ring_method.ini, coarse_space_type msgfem_ring) in the 3D weak
# scaling at velocity 100, 216 subdomains: extension from_ring (as analysed in the paper, for the talk) and
# from_boundary (cheaper, not analysed; for reference). Same options and layout as weak3d_rep_P216.
ringmethod_fr_ws3_v100_P216 ex6 ex6.ini,methods/ring_method.ini -problem.coefficient channels -problem.kappa_channel 1e6 -problem.velocity_scale 100 -grid.cells "24 24 24" -solver.maxit 1000 -solver.restart 1000 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.threshold 0.5 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.nev 8 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.ncv 64 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.nev_max 600
ringmethod_fb_ws3_v100_P216 ex6 ex6.ini,methods/ring_method.ini -problem.coefficient channels -problem.kappa_channel 1e6 -problem.velocity_scale 100 -grid.cells "24 24 24" -solver.maxit 1000 -solver.restart 1000 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.threshold 0.5 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.nev 8 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.ncv 64 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.nev_max 600 -prec.coarse_space.extension from_boundary
# Threshold 0.01 for MS-GFEM-ring: its spectrum has the same cluster of small eigenvalues as MS-GFEM (the channels),
# but then a dense part from ~0.4 on (like GenEO), so 0.5 over-selects (216 subdomains: 4.2x the coarse dimension).
# Locally (8 subdomains) any threshold in 0.005..0.1 gives the coarse space of MS-GFEM at 0.5; 0.01 is round and in the gap.
ringmethod_fr_tau0.01_ws3_v100_P216 ex6 ex6.ini,methods/ring_method.ini -problem.coefficient channels -problem.kappa_channel 1e6 -problem.velocity_scale 100 -grid.cells "24 24 24" -solver.maxit 1000 -solver.restart 1000 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.threshold 0.01 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.nev 8 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.ncv 64 -prec.coarse_space.eigensolver.nev_max 600
