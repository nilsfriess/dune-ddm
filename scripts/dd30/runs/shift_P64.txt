# Shift of the eigensolver for the MS-GFEM variants whose projection lies outside of the shifted solve (two_sided,
# projected, ring): in elliptic_P64.txt (shift -1e-3) they needed 67 / 103 / 86 iterations on the random coefficient
# where saddle_point (exact shift-invert) needed 21, although for a symmetric problem all solve the same eigenproblem.
# The high-contrast modes have eigenvalues ~1e-6, far below the shift. Same runs with shifts -1e-6 and -1e-9
