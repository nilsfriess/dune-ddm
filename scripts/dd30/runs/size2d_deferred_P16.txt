# GenEO and the saddle point method of size2d_P16.txt again, with the fine-level factorisation deferred to after the
# coarse space (3320310). With it in memory during the eigensolve, both were OOM-killed at 512^2 cells per subdomain
# (16 ranks on one node). The 256^2 runs check that iterations and times are unchanged and show the lower peak memory.
