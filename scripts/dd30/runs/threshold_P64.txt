# Thresholds instead of a fixed number of eigenvectors, on the channels problem (fixed in space, ex6.ini defaults:
# 16 rods per slice, width 1/32) with 64 ranks, 64^3 elements, contrast 1e6. One round threshold per method family:
# the two-sided variants (inner_product subdomain and ring) have much smaller eigenvalues than the saddle point
# method. 0.01 lies in their spectral gap (weak_P64 nev 80 spectra). For the saddle point, its spectrum on this
# problem (nev 80, printed) decides between the round candidates 0.5 and 1. nev 8 is the minimum, nev_max 400 the
# maximum per subdomain (the Lanczos method warns if it is reached)
