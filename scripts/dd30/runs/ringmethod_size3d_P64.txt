# The MS-GFEM-ring method (methods/ring_method.ini) in the 3D size ladder (as size3d_rep_P64, 12^3..24^3 elements per
# subdomain, 64 subdomains, RANKS_PER_NODE=8): extension from_ring and from_boundary. Its ring (the overlap and three
# layers of the subdomain) is most of a small subdomain; does the method get cheaper relative to the others as the
# subdomains grow?
# Threshold 0.01 for MS-GFEM-ring: its spectrum has the same cluster of small eigenvalues as MS-GFEM (the channels),
# but then a dense part from ~0.4 on (like GenEO), so 0.5 over-selects (216 subdomains: 4.2x the coarse dimension).
# Locally (8 subdomains) any threshold in 0.005..0.1 gives the coarse space of MS-GFEM at 0.5; 0.01 is round and in the gap.
